# Centient

A personal money tracker: you log what you spend, earn and move, and Centient shows where it stands and where it is going.

## Language

### Money

**Entry**:
One thing you logged, such as an expense, an income, a transfer or a split bill. It can touch several accounts or pockets.
_Avoid_: Transaction, record

**Pocket**:
A named share of one account's money, such as Rent or Holiday. Every account has a Main pocket.
_Avoid_: Envelope, jar

**Goal**:
A pocket with a target amount, and optionally a target date.
_Avoid_: Savings target

**Budget**:
A spending limit for each cycle, on a category, a pocket or both, in one currency. The same category can have a budget in each currency.
_Avoid_: Allowance, cap

**Temporary budget**:
A budget for a fixed stretch of days instead of a cycle, such as 14 days from today. It ends at midnight after its last day and moves to a history. Spending inside it also counts toward the cycle budget.
_Avoid_: Trip budget, short budget

**Cycle**:
The stretch of time from your month start day to the day before the next one. It is the app's only meaning of "month". A cycle is named by the month that holds most of its days: start day 1–15 takes the month it starts in, 16–31 the month it ends in.
_Avoid_: Month, billing period

### People

**Person**:
Someone you split a bill or an installment with. They need no app; they exist only in your workspace, as a tab.
_Avoid_: Friend, contact, party

**Tab**:
The running tally of what a person owes you, such as "Andi owes me 20k". Only you see it. You settle it by logging a transfer.
_Avoid_: IOU account (in the UI), debt

**Share**:
The part of a bill that belongs to one person. Shares start equal; you can type over one, and the people you have not edited split the rest.
_Avoid_: Portion, cut

**Pot**:
A gift or cost several people pay into, run by one of them. When you join someone else's pot you log only your share, with the pot's total, headcount and who runs it.
_Avoid_: Patungan (in the UI)

### Insights

**Insights**:
The screen that reads your past entries back to you: trends, comparisons and the report.
_Avoid_: Reports (as a screen name), Statistics

**Analytics**:
The part of Insights about your spending trends. It is about your money, not about how the app is used.
_Avoid_: Stats, telemetry

**Report**:
A file or printout of one period's Insights that you take out of the app.
_Avoid_: Export (that word stays for the full CSV of entries)

**Spending**:
Money that left you for good: expenses, fees, and corrections filed under a category. For a split bill, only your share. Moving money between your own accounts or pockets is not spending.
_Avoid_: Outflow, expenses (when meaning the total)

**Income**:
Money that came to you from outside, excluding opening balances and balance adjustments.
_Avoid_: Inflow, earnings

**Budget forecast**:
Where a budget's spending would end by the close of the cycle if you kept spending at the speed so far. It stays hidden for the first few days of every cycle, because a few early purchases make it jump.
_Avoid_: Pace, projection

**Savings rate**:
Income minus spending, as a share of income, for one currency and one period.
_Avoid_: Saved percentage

**Daily average spendings**:
What you spend on a typical Monday, Tuesday and so on: each weekday's spending divided by how many of that weekday have passed in the period, so a weekday that came round twice does not look bigger.
_Avoid_: Weekday total, average day of the week, Daily wallet spending (the old title)

**Counted amount**:
The part of an entry that counts as spending: your share of a split bill, or only the fee of a transfer. Lists opened from Insights show it, with the whole entry explained underneath, so they add up to the number you tapped.
_Avoid_: Net amount, real amount
